# VTM editorial artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool for this website. Conceptual
illustrations, not photographs of customers, events, publications or study data.
Original generated PNGs were resized to 1000px wide and encoded as WebP at quality
83 for web delivery. The originals remain in the generation library.

Shared visual brief: premium editorial still-life imagery for a modern German
insurance technology magazine. Tactile ivory paper and warm stone, cobalt and
ink navy, sea-glass green, restrained amber accents, sunlight and architectural
shadows. Landscape 3:2 compositions, no lettering, logos, people or watermark.

- `editorial-editorial.webp`: sculptural fanned ivory magazine pages, a cobalt
  circular disc and an amber glass sphere. Real paper texture, refined editorial
  art direction. Concept illustration rather than an actual publication.
- `editorial-podcast.webp`: dark metal broadcast microphone on a slim stand,
  curved cobalt acoustic forms and a translucent amber disc. Ink-navy studio,
  warm rim light, tactile material detail.
- `editorial-dialog.webp`: overlapping translucent green and cobalt speech
  forms on stepped ivory stone, with a small amber hemisphere. Perspectives
  coming together; sophisticated natural light and glass textures.
- `editorial-research.webp`: five translucent cobalt and sea-glass-green columns
  of different heights on warm ivory stone, with an amber sphere. A conceptual
  representation of insights; no numerical values, axes or actual research data.
