# Frühere Formatbilder

Active images are generic symbolic photos generated with the built-in image generation tool, not actual VTM employees, studios, webinars or studies. Each carries the caption “Symbolbild · KI-generiert”. The 25 EXPERTS card uses the existing official partner logo. WebP assets are resized to 1000px at quality 82; original PNGs remain in the generation library. Former editorial-*.webp still lifes remain as unused legacy assets.

Brand reference: https://www.versicherungstech-magazin.de/, inspected 9 November 2026. Observed core colors: #0A1638 (navy), #1F4FA3 (cobalt), #FFFFFF (white), #F3F7FD (pale blue). Bricolage Grotesque is used for headings, Instrument Sans for editorial components. Local font files and OFL licenses are included in fonts/.

### format-article.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: website offer thumbnail. A realistic documentary editorial photograph of a journalist's working desk in a modern German business newsroom. A hand types on a slim laptop, beside a simple unbranded notebook with handwritten notes and a dark blue pen. Screen shows a blurred magazine article layout with no readable words. Natural cool daylight, white desk, navy clothing, restrained cobalt blue details. Close horizontal framing on hands, laptop and notebook; no faces. Credible contemporary media photography, no art installation, no sculptures, no abstract shapes, no gold or amber objects, no beige styling, no brand logos, no legible text. Landscape 3:2. A generic symbolic image, not a real VTM employee or office.
